  5. Self-improvement. Check out the following 6 tips to a happier you!

    • Exercising Regularly:Everyone knows exercise is a cornerstone of health and fitness - but did you know that even just a few minutes of daily exercise can dramatically boost your ability to be happier?
      Action Steps: Aim to have combination of aerobic, stretch and jogging exercises between 30-60 minutes everyday.

    • Expressing Gratitude:Multiple peer-reviewed studies have shown that frequently expressing gratitude increases happiness and fulfillment, reduces stress and depression, and even relieves the symptoms of illness.
      Action Steps: Keep a gratitude journal and write down at least one thing you're grateful for at the start of each day.

    • Getting Enough Sleep:Multiple studies show that your quality of sleep directly affects your quality of life. A lack of sleep wreaks havoc on your brain in many ways, including your amygdala's ability to control mood swings, brain tissue deterioration, and the buildup of adenosine, a fatigue-inducing chemical.
      Action Steps: Allow yourself to have at least 7 hours of quality sleep each night.

    • Focusing On Relationships:"We are happy when we have family, we are happy when we have friends and almost all the other things we think make us happy are actually just ways of getting more family and friends."
      And remember - 'not staying in touch with my loved ones' is one of the biggest regrets of the elderly.'
      Action Steps: remember to stay in touch with your family and friendly regularly!

    • Meditation:Meditation has been shown to correspond with positive changes in you. Making you A calmer, more positive and happier you, not just when you're meditating, but throughout your day.
      Action Steps: Start meditating for at least 5 minutes a day, either in the morning before you start your day, or in the evening before bed - preferably both! The more time you spend with meditation, the more likely you will be able to achieve happiness fully.

    • Eat sensibly:Reduce portions,eat slowly, Make snacks healthy (Avoid high fat or high sugar snacks such as; Cookies,Potato chips and regular sodas), Reduce Fat Intake, Drink Plenty of Water.
      Action Steps:Check out this website to build a healthy plate!
